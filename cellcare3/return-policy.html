Return & Warranty Policy

1. Transit Damage or Wrong Item Replacement

If any EV spare part, cell, tool, or battery component arrives physically damaged or differs from your confirmed order, please share a complete unboxing video within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp (+91 85059 10581) or email (CellCareIndia2025@gmail.com).

2. Quality & Testing Assurance

All EV parts, NMC cells, and battery testing instruments are inspected before dispatch. Warranty terms vary by product category and are clearly stated on your confirmed WhatsApp quotation and GST invoice.

3. Exclusions

Physical impact damage, improper polarity wiring, water damage, or unauthorized modification are excluded from replacement coverage.
