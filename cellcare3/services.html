Academy Service 02

Practical Lithium Battery Repair Training

Learn real-world EV & lithium battery repair directly inside our active New Delhi workshop. No theory-only slides — get hands-on bench experience diagnosing live battery packs with professional tools.

What You Will Practice Hands-On:

  • EV Battery Diagnosis & Troubleshooting: Identifying weak series groups, voltage sag, moisture corrosion, and charger faults.
  • BMS Testing & Replacement: Wiring 13S/16S/20S hardware & Smart Bluetooth BMS boards, MOSFET & thermal sensor checks.
  • Cell Testing & IR Grading: Measuring internal resistance (RC-3563 / industrial testers), capacity grading, and IR matching.
  • Safe Battery Opening & Repair Practices: Safe enclosure teardown, nickel strip removal, pulse spot welding, insulation & resealing.
  • Safety & Real Pack Case Studies: Working on LiFePO4 & NMC scooter and E-Rickshaw battery packs safely.
Upcoming Batches & Availability:
New practical batches start every month in New Delhi (Weekday & Weekend options available). Seats are limited per batch so every participant gets direct workbench tool access.
