Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I place an order on Cell Care India?

Simply browse our live product catalog, select your required quantity, and click Enquire on WhatsApp on any product page (or add multiple items to your Enquiry Cart and submit via Checkout). WhatsApp will open pre-filled with your product name, SKU, quantity, and price to our official desk at +91 85059 10581.

2. Are product prices and availability updated live?

Yes! Our catalog syncs dynamically with our live inventory sheet so product names, SKUs, prices, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and stock status are always up to date.

3. How are orders paid and dispatched across India?

All orders are confirmed directly via WhatsApp (+91 85059 10581) on a prepaid dispatch basis. Once confirmed, parts ship from New Delhi via Delhivery, BlueDart, or VRL Surface Cargo with full tracking details.

4. Do you provide GST invoices for wholesale / dealer purchases?

Yes, GST-compliant tax invoices are provided for retail and B2B wholesale buyers. Simply share your GSTIN when sending your WhatsApp order enquiry.

5. What is your return & replacement policy?

Please check our Return & Warranty Policy for full details on unboxing verification and replacement coverage.
